## New references and access limits

- **Graham, D.H. (1939).** *Breeding habits of the fishes of Otago Harbour and adjacent seas*. Transactions and Proceedings of the Royal Society of New Zealand **69**, 361–372. National Library's [Papers Past full text](https://paperspast.natlib.govt.nz/periodicals/TPRSNZ1940-69.2.5.24) inspected, *Hippocampus abdominalis* subsection. The archive groups the volume under 1940, but the article records separate issue in December 1939. This small historical observation supports approximate timing, not a modern population distribution.
- **Woods, C.M.C. (2005).** *Reproductive output of male seahorses, Hippocampus abdominalis, from Wellington Harbour, New Zealand: implications for conservation*. New Zealand Journal of Marine and Freshwater Research **39**(4), **881–888**. [DOI 10.1080/00288330.2005.9517359](https://doi.org/10.1080/00288330.2005.9517359). Publisher-indexed abstract inspected; direct open returned 403. Use the verified 881–888 pagination rather than the apparent 811–888 typo in Fishes of Australia's reference list.
- **IUCN Seahorse, Pipefish & Seadragon Specialist Group.** Its [global assessment page](https://www.iucn-seahorse.org/iucn-global-assessments), marked updated August 11, 2025, identifies the group as a Red List Authority. Its species table updated September 2026 is the recent authority-owned corroboration retrieved here. The [older scoping table](https://www.iucn-seahorse.org/global-scoping) also says LC but is explicitly dated September 9, **2021**; it should not be substituted for the newer page.

Woods 2000 remains a useful original breeding-study citation ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/00288330.2000.9516950)), but this follow-up obtained only its abstract, not its gestation tables. No precise incubation range is newly attributed to that unread full paper. The new sources do not change the previously documented CITES access boundary.
