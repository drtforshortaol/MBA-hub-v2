# EVO V2 — Timeline-only Scientific Evidence Review Draft 1
Date: 2026-10-14
Status: Literature screening; no scientific approvals and no changes to Timeline or Master Map.

## Review principle
A dated fossil occurrence is a minimum evidence constraint, not automatically the true evolutionary origin. A proposed molecular-clock estimate is not an observed fossil date. Distinguish speculative hypotheses, trace fossils, definitive body fossils, lineage origins, and later diversification.

| 7 Origin of life | Perhaps 4.3–4.2 Ga (?) | Potential biogenic graphite in 4.1-Ga zircon is controversial as proof; microfossils ~3.5 Ga are stronger. A 2026 review emphasizes uncertainty in earlier potential biosignatures. | High uncertainty; do not turn hypothetical date into confirmed origin |
