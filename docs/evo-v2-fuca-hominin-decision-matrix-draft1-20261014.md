## Sources and competing interpretations
**FUCA**:
- *The Origin(s) of Cell(s): Pre-Darwinian Evolution from FUCAs to LUCA* (2021), Journal of Molecular Evolution, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00239-021-10014-4 — distinguishes theorized communal FUCAs from LUCA and notes terminology differences.
- *The origin of modern terrestrial life* (2008), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2640990/ — explicitly distinguishes LUCA from the first cell and notes earlier lineages may have existed.
- *Looking for the Last Universal Common Ancestor* (2012), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3899962/ — explores limitations of assigning a single organism to universal ancestry.

**Hominin divergence**:
- Smithsonian Human Origins: *How Do We Know Humans Are Primates?*, https://humanorigins.si.edu/education/how-do-we-know/how-do-we-know-humans-are-primates — gives 8–6 Ma as broad educational framing.
- Patterson et al. (2006), *Nature*, https://www.nature.com/articles/nature04789 — presents a different genomic estimate and possible complex speciation, demonstrating model dependence; not a current consensus by itself.
- *Evidence of a chimpanzee-sized ancestor of humans but a gibbon-sized ancestor of apes* (2017), Nature Communications, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-00997-4 — describes fossil sparsity, possible basal hominins and divergent date estimates.
