## Initial source findings — 2026-11-12

- **Giant kelp nomenclature is contested across references.** WoRMS lists *Macrocystis tenuifolia* as accepted under *M. pyrifera*: https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?p=taxlist&tName=Macrocystis . A 2026 population-genomics paper discusses the northern *M. tenuifolia* treatment while noting unresolved genetic relationships: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13373871/ . The 2024 Seafood Watch expert-review draft likewise notes a north-of-Point-Conception *M. tenuifolia* recommendation that was not widely implemented: https://www.seafoodwatch.org/globalassets/sfw/pdf/expert-review/2024/110424/seafood-watch-kelp-bull-giant-bc-28332.pdf . **Disposition:** conflicting taxonomy; keep draft label and visible caveat pending a named reviewer and selected taxonomic authority.
- **Wakame origin/invasiveness:** California Invasive Plant Council identifies *Undaria pinnatifida* as introduced, locally invasive in disturbed California estuaries, with limited statewide ecological impacts: https://www.cal-ipc.org/plants/profile/undaria-pinnatifida-profile/ . UC Agriculture and Natural Resources includes it among reportable invasive seaweeds: https://ucanr.edu/site/california-aquatic-invasive-species-ais . **Disposition:** evidence supports the introduced label and a geographically qualified invasive note, not a claim of statewide severe impact or presence everywhere. Final sign-off pending.

These are source-backed research notes, **not Monterey Bay Aquarium scientific approval**.

## Additional evidence — 2026-11-12
- **Sea palm (*Postelsia palmaeformis*):** California Department of Fish and Wildlife describes an annual species on strongly wave-exposed mid/high intertidal rocks, with a California range south to Point Buchon. NOAA Olympic Coast also describes extreme wave exposure. Sources: https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Kelp and https://olympiccoast.noaa.gov/living/marinelife/seaweed/ . Current draft habitat and appearance are consistent with these references; review remains pending.
- **Winged kelp (*Alaria marginata*):** CDFW documents low-intertidal to shallow-subtidal rocky habitat, and NOAA Olympic Coast identifies the species as winged kelp. Note: CDFW also calls *A. marginata* “wakame,” a common-name collision with the introduced *Undaria pinnatifida*. Keep scientific names visible to avoid confusion. Sources: https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Kelp and https://olympiccoast.noaa.gov/living/marinelife/seaweed/ .
- **Sea cabbage name requires reconciliation:** CDFW uses *Saccharina sessilis* for sea cabbage, while this draft uses *Hedophyllum sessile*. Determine the current accepted combination and synonym relationship before publishing. Source: https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Kelp .

## Sea cabbage taxonomy reconciliation — 2026-11-12
- **World Register of Marine Species (WoRMS)** lists *Saccharina sessilis* as accepted under *Hedophyllum sessile*, supporting the app's existing name: https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?p=taxlist&tName=Saccharina .
- **NCBI Taxonomy** uses *Saccharina sessilis* as its current name and records *Hedophyllum sessile* as a homotypic synonym: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=416828 . This is a database-treatment difference, not evidence of two separate sea-cabbage species.
- **NOAA technical report** uses *Hedophyllum sessile*, formerly *Saccharina sessilis*, and describes a stipe-less sea cabbage extending south to Monterey County: https://swfsc-publications.fisheries.noaa.gov/publications/CR/2025/2025Purcell3.pdf .
- **Disposition:** retain *Hedophyllum sessile* in the app (WoRMS/NOAA convention); add *Saccharina sessilis* as a search synonym. Final scientific sign-off still required.

## Bull kelp verification — 2026-11-12
- **Accepted name:** WoRMS lists *Nereocystis luetkeana* as accepted: https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?id=240752&p=taxdetails .
- **Habitat and range:** California Department of Fish and Wildlife describes an annual canopy-forming alga attached to rocky substrate, most dominant north of Davenport, with a North American range south to Point Conception: https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Kelp/Commonly-Harvested-Marine-Algae .
- **Life cycle:** Korabik et al. (2025), *Algae* 40(2):117–125, found no evidence for a proposed bypass of the microscopic gametophyte stage; established biphasic life history remains supported: https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/71811 .
- **Disposition:** app's name, single terminal float, canopy grouping, and typical annual habit are consistent with these references; Aquarium scientific reviewer approval remains pending.

## Additional CDFW verification — 2026-11-12
- **Stiff-stiped kelp (*Laminaria setchellii*):** CDFW calls this species kombu and documents perennial growth on rocky low-intertidal to subtidal substrates from Alaska to Baja California. This independently supports habitat/longevity, **not** the app's detailed stipe identification trait. Source: https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Kelp/Commonly-Harvested-Marine-Algae .
- **Sea cabbage (*Hedophyllum sessile* / *Saccharina sessilis*):** CDFW documents perennial growth, rocky mid-intertidal to shallow subtidal habitat, and range south to Point Sur. It uses *Saccharina sessilis*; the naming-authority difference remains disclosed. Same source.
- **Common-name warning:** CDFW explicitly notes that native *Alaria marginata* and introduced *Undaria pinnatifida* may both be called 'wakame.' Scientific names should remain prominent in the app. Same source.

## Additional research — 2026-11-12 (UCSC / Stanford / Greater Farallones)
- **Feather boa kelp:** UCSC MARINe identifies *Egregia menziesii* by straplike rachis with many lateral blades and marginal floats, and documents rocky low intertidal habitat and variation among northern/southern populations. https://marine.ucsc.edu/target/egregia/ .
- **Stalked kelp:** Stanford SeaNet describes *Pterygophora californica* as having a single tall stipe with lateral blades, distinct from *Eisenia arborea*'s Y-shaped split. Greater Farallones Association describes *Pterygophora* as long-lived perennial. https://seanet.stanford.edu/Ochrophyta and https://farallones.org/sanctuary-wildlife/algae/ .
- **Southern sea palm:** Stanford SeaNet describes *Eisenia arborea* with a Y-shaped stipe bifurcation and toothed blades, including a disjunct Monterey-area distribution. https://seanet.stanford.edu/Ochrophyta .
- **Common-name collision:** Greater Farallones Association calls *Pterygophora californica* 'winged kelp' while the app calls *Alaria marginata* winged kelp. Keep scientific names prominently displayed; verify guide-specific common-name conventions before final publication.
