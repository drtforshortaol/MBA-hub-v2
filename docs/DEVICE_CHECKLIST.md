# Foundation and release checklist

## First architecture proof

- Host developer and tester bundles at distinct V2 URLs; confirm manifest scopes and cache prefixes, and leave V1 unchanged.
- Open each proof app; confirm the approved layouts, all collapsed sections, image proportions and navigation.
- Search a principle or sensory alias; open a result directly at its expanded section; Back must return to the search and query.
- Wait for Ready offline. Close the Hub. Disable Wi-Fi and cellular data, restart the installed Hub, open both apps and the image.
- Install a changed build. Confirm that unchanged files are reused, new content appears after closing old sessions, and interruption leaves the prior release usable.
- Verify that a tester cannot reveal developer controls or invoke repair through direct worker messages.
- Retain and redeploy the prior complete bundle; verify rollback after restarting the installed PWA.

## Before migrating the rest

Owner reviews the first proof. Resolve blocking defects. Confirm the separate repository and hosting privacy choices. Inventory source content, media permissions, local data and cross-links for each of the remaining 23 applications. Keep original dated notes and attribution. Independent EVO and Library keep separate ownership; offline use needs an explicit approved distribution/interface.

## Before full volunteer release

- All required applications migrated or approved independent integrations verified.
- Complete navigation, urgent call actions, source-note deep links, images and scientific-content review.
- Permission and privacy review of every published asset.
- Offline completeness, fresh install, already-installed update, interrupted preparation and recovery on actual iPhones.
- Owner and three trusted reviewers complete the pilot; feedback stays informal.
- Full compatible artifact retained; deployed rollback demonstrated.
- Owner explicitly approves the full V2 rollout. V1 remains available.
