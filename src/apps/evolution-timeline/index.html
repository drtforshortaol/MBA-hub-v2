How to read the app

Story by Groups keeps related evolutionary threads together so the audience can follow the organelles and lineages. Strict Time Map ignores grouping and sorts key milestones by date. Learning Tools offers four big-picture summaries. Nothing is removed: detailed explanations remain in Learn More.
