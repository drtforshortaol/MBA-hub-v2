Wildlife Rescue

Quick response • Monterey Bay

First: protect people and wildlife.

Keep children and pets back. Do not touch, feed, pour water on, move or attempt to rescue marine mammals. Observe from a safe distance and call a trained responder. For an immediate threat to human life, call 911.

Who should I call?

🐦 Birds & land wildlife831-264-5427SPCA Monterey County 🦭 Seals & sea lions415-289-7325Marine Mammal Center 🦦 Sea otters · Monterey County831-648-4840Monterey Bay Aquarium 🐋 Entangled whales877-767-9425NOAA SOS-WHAL

Tap a number to call. Do not attempt disentanglement. If the species is uncertain, describe the animal to the responder.

🧭 What to say when you call

“I'm reporting a possibly injured or distressed animal.”

  1. Where: exact location, nearby landmark or GPS.
  2. What: animal type, approximate size and how many.
  3. Condition: alive/dead, injury, abnormal behavior, entanglement, oil or fishing gear.
  4. When: time last observed and direction of travel if moving.
  5. How to reach you: name and callback number.

Photos/video can help only if taken without approaching or disturbing the animal. Follow responder directions.

🦭 Seals & sea lions — symptoms & response

The Marine Mammal Center

📞 415-289-7325

Report obviously injured, stranded or ill pinnipeds. Do not approach or handle. A resting seal on a beach is not necessarily stranded.

Warning signs in the July Naturalist Field Note

Excessive drooling or foaming, seizures, side-to-side head motion, disorientation, and unusual upward/backward head posture (“stargazing”). These warrant a call; do not diagnose from appearance alone.

Keep observers and dogs well back. NOAA's stranding safety guidance says to maintain at least 100 yards when possible.

🦦 Sea otters — Monterey County

Monterey Bay Aquarium Sea Otter Hotline

📞 831-648-4840

Report apparently abandoned pups, injured, ill or deceased sea otters in Monterey County. Stay at least 50 feet away; do not touch, feed or attempt capture. Note exact location and behavior.

Elsewhere: Santa Cruz County and north, or San Luis Obispo County and south — Marine Mammal Center 415-289-7325 for live distressed otters. Dead otters outside Monterey County may be routed to CDFW.

🐋 Whales, dolphins & entanglement

NOAA Fisheries

Entangled whale:

📞 877-767-9425 (SOS-WHAL)

Stranded, injured or deceased marine mammal:

📞 866-767-6114

⚠️ Oiled wildlife or suspected rabies

Oiled wildlife

📞 877-823-6926 · Oiled Wildlife Care Network

Do not attempt to clean an oiled animal yourself.

Wild animal suspected of rabies

Do not handle. For a living animal, call SPCA Wildlife Center 831-264-5427. If the animal contacted a pet or is deceased, Monterey County Animal Services: 831-769-8850.

📍 Additional local contacts & situations

Deceased whale, dolphin, seal, sea lion or turtle — Monterey County south of Pajaro River: Moss Landing Marine Laboratories 831-771-4422. Confirm routing with a wildlife responder if unsure.

Dead seabirds: NOAA advises leaving them in place on sanctuary beaches unless instructed otherwise; report unusual mortality events or suspected oil contamination.

Roadside deceased animals: Monterey County Public Works 831-755-4800.
